# 📁 Medical Coding Case Studies

Below are practical medical coding scenarios including ICD-10, CPT, and HCPCS codes.

---

## 🩺 Case 1: Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)
**Patient:** 60-year-old female  
**Symptoms:** Fever, chills, urinary pain  
**Procedure:** Urinalysis  
**Diagnosis:** UTI  
**Treatment:** Injection of Ceftriaxone 1g

**Codes:**
- ICD-10: `N39.0` – Urinary tract infection, site not specified
- CPT: `0371U` – Urinalysis (Lab Test)
- HCPCS: `J0696` – Injection, Ceftriaxone sodium, per 250 mg

---

## 🦶 Case 2: Fracture After Injury
**Patient:** 25-year-old male  
**Symptoms:** Ankle swelling after trauma  
**Procedure:** X-ray (2 views)  
**Diagnosis:** Ankle Fracture  
**Treatment:** Walking boot

**Codes:**
- ICD-10: `S82.891A` – Other fracture of lower leg, initial encounter
- CPT: `70250` – Radiologic exam (X-ray), lower extremity
- HCPCS: `L4360` – Walking boot

---

## 💉 Case 3: Malaria Diagnosis
**Patient:** 30-year-old male  
**Symptoms:** High fever, chills, fatigue  
**Procedure:** Blood smear  
**Diagnosis:** Malaria

**Codes:**
- ICD-10: `B52.9` – Plasmodium malariae malaria, unspecified
- CPT: `87207` – Blood smear, malaria
